Image quality matters

How to take a useful plant photo

A clear image helps CropCare Sathi identify visible patterns such as spots, yellowing, curling, holes, powdery growth, and wilting.

  1. 1 Take the image in natural daylight when possible.
  2. 2 Keep the affected leaf, stem, fruit, or plant in focus.
  3. 3 Avoid dark, blurry, distant, or heavily filtered photos.
  4. 4 Capture both close-up symptoms and a wider plant view.
  5. 5 Add notes about crop name, rainfall, watering, pests, and timing.

Important reminder

When you should contact a local expert

Seek local agriculture-extension or plant-health support if symptoms spread rapidly, affect many plants, cause major wilting or crop loss, or appear after pesticide or fertilizer use. An image result should support—not replace—field inspection and local expert guidance.
